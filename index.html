FAQ

Questions Newark drivers ask us most

What does "Same Day Car Approval" actually mean?+
It means you can walk in, choose a vehicle, and get a financing decision from us directly the same day — without waiting on a bank to review your application.
Can I get approved with bad credit or no credit?+
Yes. As a Buy Here Pay Here dealership, we finance in-house and welcome all credit situations, including bad credit, no credit, or past repossessions.
Do I need to go through a bank to get financed?+
No. Our no bank financing model means the dealership itself is the lender, so approval doesn't depend on outside bank criteria.
What documents do I need to apply?+
Generally a valid driver's license, proof of income, and proof of residence. Our team will confirm exactly what's needed for your situation when you call or visit.
Is your team able to help Spanish-speaking customers?+
Yes, we have Spanish speaking staff on-site to walk you through every step of the car buying and financing process.
